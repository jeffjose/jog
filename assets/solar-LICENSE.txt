The icons in solar.ttf are from Solar, by 480 Design, in its "linear"
style, as published by Iconify (@iconify-json/solar).

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made here: the line is drawn 2.25 wide where Solar draws it at
1.5, the lines are turned into filled outlines, and the icons jog uses
are gathered into a font (tools/solar_font.py).
